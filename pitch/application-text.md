# Cintrifuse Emerging Founder Residency — Application Text
**Urban Hikers | Local Pulse OS**

---

## 1. What is your company / idea? (Elevator pitch — 2–3 sentences)

Urban Hikers is a hyper-local city intelligence platform that connects foot traffic to real-time deals, events, and civic energy through NFC tap points placed around urban neighborhoods. When someone taps a node or scans a QR code, they instantly see what's buzzing within walking distance — flash deals, live performances, crowdsourced vibe checks, curated walking routes, and civic signals, all AI-enriched in real time. Local businesses pay to broadcast on the network; the city becomes the interface.

---

## 2. What problem are you solving?

There are two problems happening simultaneously on every urban street corner:

**For walkers and visitors:** Discovery is broken. Google Maps shows you what exists. Yelp shows you what was reviewed months ago. Nothing tells you what's *happening right now* within 500 feet of where you're standing. Urban explorers — whether locals on a lunch break or visitors on their first day — have no ambient, real-time pulse on the city around them.

**For local businesses:** Hyperlocal reach is out of their budget. Digital ads target demographics, not proximity. A coffee shop, gallery, or food truck can't reliably reach the person walking past their door at 2pm on a Tuesday. The tools that exist (social media, Yelp ads, Google My Business) are designed for search intent, not foot traffic conversion.

Urban Hikers solves both sides with one infrastructure layer: NFC Sector Hub Nodes that create a two-sided real-time signal network — walkers get the city's pulse, businesses get a direct broadcast channel to nearby foot traffic.

---

## 3. Who are your customers and how do you make money?

**Customer 1 — Local Businesses (B2B, primary revenue)**
Restaurants, bars, boutiques, galleries, event venues, and food trucks in walkable urban districts. They pay monthly subscription fees to broadcast flash deals, events, and live offers through the network.

- Standard tier: Base listing + broadcast access
- Premium tier: Featured placement + real-time analytics dashboard
- Anchor tier: Exclusive node sponsorship + deep analytics + co-branded hub presence

**Customer 2 — Event Organizers & Civic Partners (B2B, secondary)**
Festivals, conferences, civic organizations, and BIDs (Business Improvement Districts) purchase Flash Rotation slots — prominent timed banner placements that reach all active sessions in a geographic zone during their event window.

**Customer 3 — Urban Explorers (B2C, free)**
End users pay nothing. Their taps, tab views, vibe reports, and engagement data create the analytics that make the B2B product valuable. More walkers = more valuable network.

**Revenue model:** Recurring SaaS subscriptions (partner tiers) + transactional ad inventory (Flash Rotation slots). No transaction fees, no freemium gating — businesses pay for reach, not results.

## 4. What is your traction so far?

- **Working MVP:** Full-stack platform is live — NFC tap ingestion, real-time broadcast feed, crowdsourced vibe checks (chill / buzzing / packed), curated walking tours, points of interest layer, partner management, and a super admin analytics dashboard tracking taps, tab views, and impressions
- **AI integration:** Gemini API powers civic signal ingestion, enriching public event data with walking relevance scores, vibe predictions, and short-form descriptions
- **Beta users:** Real users have tested the tap-to-feed flow with direct and QR access vectors; session analytics are actively logging
- **Infrastructure:** Firebase/Firestore backend with production-grade security rules, rate limiting, CORS configuration, and role-based access control (admin, partner, partner_admin, partner_viewer, content_editor)
- **Location:** Cincinnati-based — no relocation, no ramp-up time, ready to deploy nodes day one

## 5. Why Cincinnati? Why now?

Cincinnati is the ideal launch city for Urban Hikers — and not by accident.

OTR is one of the most walkable, densely activated urban neighborhoods in the Midwest. The Riverfront, Washington Park, Findlay Market, and the Main Street corridor are already magnets for foot traffic. What's missing is a real-time intelligence layer that connects that foot traffic to what's happening at street level.

Cincinnati also has a business community that's been underserved by hyperlocal digital tools. The small businesses anchoring OTR don't have marketing teams — they need a turnkey broadcast channel that works the moment someone walks by. Urban Hikers is built for exactly that operator.

Beyond OTR: the city is investing in pedestrian infrastructure, the streetcar corridor creates a natural node backbone, and Cintrifuse's own Union Hall is the kind of anchor destination that belongs at the center of the network.

I'm already here. The first nodes deploy in Cincinnati. The story starts in this city.
